## CORE THESIS

NUMU is not a product company.

It is a **bio-composites platform** building the GCC’s first regional system for growing and pressing materials from agricultural waste.

The strategy is explicit:

- Start with **high-margin specialty products**
- Build **regional production + certification infrastructure**
- Scale through **licensing and partnerships**, not factories

---

## WHY NOW (STRUCTURAL TIMING)

### 1. POLICY
- UAE Net Zero 2050 + circular procurement

- ~100% of materials imported
- No regional bio-composite manufacturing

### 3. TIMING WINDOW
- Global mycelium industry reset (failures of Bolt Threads, MycoWorks)

## THE PLATFORM

NUMU runs a **dual production system**:

### 1. GROWN
- Mycelium cultivated in molds
- Creates:
  - acoustic panels
  - complex shapes
  - premium surfaces

### 2. PRESSED
- Waste biomass → heat-pressed boards
- Fast cycle (minutes)
- Scalable production

## PLATFORM LOGIC

NUMU monetizes through **4 revenue engines**:

### ENGINE 1 — Decorative Acoustic Panels
- Immediate revenue
